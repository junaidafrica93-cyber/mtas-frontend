🔐 Privacy Policy (POPIA Compliance)

This Privacy Policy governs how My Talent Acquisition Specialist (MTAS) collects, processes, and protects personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

  • Full name and contact details
  • Email address
  • Professional information (experience, qualifications, skills)
  • Uploaded CVs and documents
  • Platform usage data and device-related information

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

All personal information is processed based on explicit user consent and where necessary for the operation of the platform.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Creating and managing user profiles
  • Providing job-matching and recruitment services
  • Improving system performance and user experience
  • Communicating relevant updates or opportunities

4. Consent

By selecting the consent checkbox during sign-up, you explicitly agree to the collection and processing of your personal information.

5. Employer Data Sharing

Your information will only be shared with potential employers if you explicitly select the “Allow sharing with employers” option.

Once shared:

  • MTAS is not responsible for how third parties use your data
  • You acknowledge that employers operate independently of MTAS

6. Employer Responsibilities

Employers receiving candidate data agree to:

  • Use data solely for recruitment purposes
  • Not store, copy, or misuse personal data beyond intended use
  • Comply with applicable data protection laws

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorised access, or misuse.

8. Data Retention

Your personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

9. User Rights (POPIA)

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to request correction
  • Right to request deletion
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

10. Account & Login Security

If account functionality is implemented:

  • You are responsible for maintaining confidentiality of login credentials
  • You must notify MTAS of any unauthorized access
  • MTAS is not liable for breaches caused by user negligence

11. Limitation of Liability

MTAS shall not be liable for:

  • Misuse of data by third-party employers
  • Unauthorized access caused by user actions
  • Indirect or consequential damages

12. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where applicable, your data may be processed in systems hosted outside South Africa, subject to appropriate safeguards.

13. Updates to Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of any updates.

14. Contact

For any privacy-related queries or requests:
